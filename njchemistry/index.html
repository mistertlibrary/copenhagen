The Assignment

New Jersey has more Superfund sites than any other state. Each one has a chemistry, a history, and people who knew more than they said. Pick one and tell its story.

Starter Sites

  • U.S. Radium, Orangeradium and the dial painters
  • Diamond Alkali, Newarkdioxin from herbicide production and the Passaic River
  • Toms Riverindustrial dumping and a cancer cluster
  • PFAS in New Jersey drinking waterthe first state standard for a “forever chemical”

How This Works

  1. Get your bearings. Start with Core Concepts: Chemistry or Science Online; identify your chemical and its properties.
  2. Dig. Search Science Reference Ultimate, GreenFile, and Newspaper Source Plus; check EPA and NJDEP site records.
  3. Write. Build your references in NoodleTools (APA) and draft your brief.

Research Briefs

A two-page brief, in APA style: the chemical and how it behaves, how it got there, who knew what and when, and what is being done now.
